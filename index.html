Questions, answered

Before you
choose.

Battery sizing and compatibility are system questions, not just product questions.

Talk to an expert ↗
Can a lithium battery work with my existing inverter?+

Potentially, but compatibility should be verified against the battery, inverter model, BMS communication and installation requirements. Voltage alone is not sufficient.

How much backup will I get?+

Runtime depends on actual load, battery energy, inverter efficiency, usable depth of discharge, surge demand and system settings. Ask us for a practical sizing estimate.

Do lithium batteries need regular maintenance?+

Many lithium systems require little routine maintenance compared with flooded lead-acid batteries, but inspection and correct operating conditions still matter.

Can I use lithium batteries with solar?+

Yes, where the battery and inverter/charger architecture supports the required charging, communication and protection behaviour.

Do you provide installation?+

Installation can include battery/inverter setup, wiring, configuration, testing and commissioning depending on the selected system and service area.
