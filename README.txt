Important replacements before launch
------------------------------------
1. Replace the demo showroom name, phone number, email, address and Google Maps link.
2. Replace all example product specs, warranty values, runtime figures and compatibility claims with verified inventory data.
3. Replace the stylized CSS product illustrations with real product photography.
4. Connect the contact form submit handler to your backend/CRM/email service.
5. Update WhatsApp links with the real WhatsApp number.
6. Add actual privacy, terms, warranty and other legal pages.
